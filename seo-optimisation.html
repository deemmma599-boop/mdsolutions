Frequently asked questions

How long does SEO take to show results?

Most websites see meaningful improvements in 3 to 6 months, depending on the site's age, competition and current technical health. Some quick wins (e.g. fixing indexing issues) can show within weeks.

Do you work with existing websites?

Yes. Our SEO service is designed specifically for existing websites. We audit what you already have and improve it — no rebuild required.

What is included in the free SEO audit?

The free audit covers technical health, indexing, page speed, mobile usability, on‑page factors, keyword opportunities and a prioritised action plan.

Can SEO help a local business?

Absolutely. Local SEO — Google Business Profile optimisation, local citations and location landing pages — helps local businesses appear in "near me" and city‑specific searches.

How much does SEO optimisation cost?

Pricing depends on the size of your site and the competitiveness of your keywords. We offer flexible monthly retainers and project‑based packages — contact us for a tailored quote.

Do you guarantee #1 rankings?

No reputable SEO agency can guarantee #1 rankings because search algorithms change constantly. We do commit to transparent reporting, measurable progress and industry best practices.
