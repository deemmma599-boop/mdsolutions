the definition

What consultation & strategy means here

Consultation and strategy is the work that happens before budget is committed — the moment where a bad decision costs weeks of wasted effort, and a good one unlocks months of progress. It is not campaign execution or vendor sales. It is independent, vendor‑neutral guidance on where to focus next, in what order, and why — grounded in what your platform actually does today.

In practice, that means sitting down with you one‑on‑one and working through the questions most teams struggle to answer on their own: Which SEO issues actually matter and which are noise? Is our current tech stack holding us back, or is it fine? Should we rebuild, redesign, or leave it alone and optimise? Where is growth realistically going to come from in the next 6–12 months, and what should we do first?

The output is a written action plan — not a slide deck — covering your SEO priorities, tech decisions and growth opportunities, sequenced by impact and effort. It's something you can hand to your own team or to another agency and act on immediately.

The value is asymmetric: a single well‑made decision can save tens of thousands in wasted development cost, or unlock a channel that outperforms everything else. That's what strategic guidance is for.

what we cover

The three pillars of every engagement

Most digital decisions touch at least one of these three areas. We work through each in depth, then connect them into one coherent plan.

SEO roadmap

What to prioritise — and in what order.

SEO advice is cheap; prioritised SEO advice is rare. We translate audit findings into a sequenced roadmap — what to fix first, what to fix next quarter, and what to ignore because the impact doesn't justify the cost.

  • Technical foundation review: indexing, crawl, Core Web Vitals, structured data.
  • Keyword and content gap analysis against your competitive set.
  • Prioritisation matrix: impact vs effort, with the reasoning behind each item.
  • 12-month roadmap aligned with realistic resource and budget.
  • Guidance on what to do in‑house vs what to outsource.

Tech stack decisions

What you have, what you need, and when to change it.

Tech stack decisions are expensive and hard to reverse. We assess whether your current CMS, hosting, frameworks and integrations are actually holding you back — and give you an honest answer about rebuild, upgrade or leave it alone.

  • CMS and platform evaluation: is the current stack fit for where you're going?
  • Build vs buy vs migrate analysis with cost and risk trade-offs.
  • Architecture review: performance, scalability, security and maintainability.
  • Integration recommendations: analytics, CRM, marketing automation, payment.
  • Hosting and infrastructure guidance aligned to traffic and growth forecast.

Digital growth strategy

Where growth is realistically going to come from.

Growth strategy is about channel choices, not more tactics. We look at your current acquisition mix, conversion funnel and competitive position, then identify the one or two bets most likely to move the needle in the next 6–12 months.

  • Channel assessment: organic, paid, email, social, referral — where the leverage is.
  • Funnel analysis: where visitors drop off and what to fix first.
  • Positioning and messaging review against the competitive set.
  • Offer and conversion strategy: pricing, CTAs, lead capture, nurture.
  • Measurement framework: what to track, how often, and what "working" looks like.

typical discussions

What we actually talk about

Real questions from real engagements. If any of these sound familiar, a consultation will help.

"Should we rebuild or optimise?"

Whether a rebuild is genuinely necessary, or whether targeted redesign, performance work and SEO fixes would deliver most of the value at a fraction of the cost.

"Is our CMS still the right choice?"

Honest evaluation of whether your current platform is holding you back — and what a migration would actually cost in time, money and SEO risk.

"Why did our rankings drop?"

Diagnosis of ranking shifts — algorithmic, technical, competitive or content — with a clear, prioritised response rather than panic.

"Where should we spend next?"

Channel and budget allocation based on likely impact, not on what any single agency happens to sell. What to double down on, what to cut.

"Do we need to hire, outsource or both?"

Building the right team and partner mix — which skills to hire for, which to retain, and how to structure the work between them.

"What should we do in the next 90 days?"

A concrete, time‑boxed action plan — not a wishlist. What to start, what to stop and what to defer, with clear reasoning for each.

faq

Consultation & strategy questions, answered

What is included in a consultation and strategy engagement?

A typical engagement includes a discovery call, a review of your current platform (SEO, analytics, tech stack and content), a structured strategy session, and a written action plan covering SEO priorities, tech decisions and growth opportunities. For retainer clients we also provide ongoing monthly guidance and reviews.

Do you implement the recommendations or just advise?

Both options exist. Some clients want pure advisory — our written roadmap, sessions and reviews — while their own team or another agency implements. Others prefer us to handle implementation of the SEO, redesign or development work as well. The choice is yours, and the advice is the same either way.

How is this different from hiring an SEO agency?

An SEO agency typically executes campaigns and reports on activity. Consulting focuses on direction: what to prioritise, why, and in which order. It is especially useful when you already have a team or agency in place, are evaluating multiple vendors, or are planning a significant investment and need an independent perspective before committing.

Who is consultation and strategy for?

It fits business owners, marketing managers, product leads and technical teams who already have a website or platform and need clarity on what to do next. It is particularly valuable before a redesign or rebuild, when SEO results have plateaued, when evaluating tech vendors, or when internal teams lack senior digital expertise.

How does a consulting engagement usually start?

It starts with a free 30‑minute discovery call to understand your platform, goals and current challenges. If there is a fit, we propose a scope — typically a one‑off audit and strategy session, or a monthly retainer. The first deliverable is always a written summary of priorities so you have something concrete to act on.

How long does it take to get a strategy roadmap?

A one‑off strategy roadmap is typically delivered within 2 to 4 weeks of the discovery call, depending on platform size and how quickly we can access analytics and existing documentation. Retainer clients receive rolling priorities each month, with quarterly deeper reviews.

Can you advise on my existing tech stack without rebuilding?

Yes. Most tech stack questions don't require a rebuild. We assess whether your current stack (CMS, hosting, frameworks, integrations) is holding you back, what can be optimised, and when a migration or upgrade genuinely makes sense versus when it is unnecessary cost. Our advice is vendor‑neutral and based on your actual bottlenecks.

How much does consultation and strategy cost?

Pricing depends on the format. A one‑off strategy session with a written roadmap is a fixed fee. Monthly retainers scale with the time commitment and complexity. We provide transparent pricing after the discovery call so there are no surprises.
