the basics

What is template redesign?

Template redesign is the upgrade of your website's interface without rebuilding the business behind it. Your template is the visual and structural layer visitors interact with — the header, navigation, hero, content blocks, forms, footer and every repeatable page layout. Over time, that layer ages: it stops matching modern screen sizes, loads slower, uses outdated patterns, and quietly leaks conversions.

A template redesign replaces that ageing layer with a modern, responsive, conversion‑focused interface — while preserving what makes your business recognisable. Your logo stays. Your brand colours stay. Your tone of voice, core messaging, services and content structure stay. What changes is the experience: spacing, hierarchy, typography, imagery treatment, interaction, performance and mobile behaviour.

It is deliberately different from a full rebrand (which redefines who you are) and from a full rebuild (which starts from zero). Template redesign is the pragmatic middle path: the fastest, lowest‑risk way to bring an existing site up to modern standards, improving usability, trust and conversion rates while protecting the SEO equity you've already earned.

At a glance

  • Keeps: logo, brand colours, tone of voice, content, URLs and SEO equity.
  • Upgrades: layout, responsiveness, speed, accessibility, navigation and conversion paths.
  • Typical timeline: 3–8 weeks depending on template count and integrations.
  • Best for: businesses with a working site that looks dated or converts poorly.
diagnosis

Signs your template is holding you back

Templates age quietly. If you recognise three or more of these, a redesign will likely pay for itself faster than you expect.

It breaks on mobile

Text overflows, buttons are too small to tap, menus don't collapse, and forms are painful to complete on a phone.

Pages load slowly

Heavy sliders, oversized images and bloated scripts push load times past three seconds — and visitors leave before seeing anything.

Visitors don't know where to click

No clear visual hierarchy, competing calls‑to‑action, and cluttered sidebars make the next step unclear.

It looks dated

Drop shadows, low‑resolution stock imagery, tiny fonts and crowded layouts signal an older, less trustworthy business.

Simple edits are hard

Changing a banner or adding a section requires a developer, so your site slowly falls out of date.

Traffic arrives, but doesn't convert

You rank and get visitors, yet enquiries and sales stay flat — a classic sign of a weak interface rather than weak marketing.

Template redesign questions, answered

What is template redesign, exactly?

Template redesign is the process of upgrading an existing website's layout, styling and interface without rebuilding the entire site or changing its brand identity. It replaces outdated, non‑responsive or low‑converting templates with modern, responsive, conversion‑focused interfaces while preserving your logo, colours, tone of voice and content structure.

Will a redesign hurt my SEO rankings?

Not when it is done correctly. We preserve URL structures, keep on‑page content and metadata, implement 301 redirects for any changed URLs, maintain heading hierarchy and internal links, and re‑test Core Web Vitals before and after launch. A well‑executed redesign usually improves SEO because of better speed, mobile usability and engagement.

Do I need to change my brand or content?

No. We keep your logo, brand colours, typography direction and messaging. Redesign means upgrading how your brand is presented — spacing, layout, hierarchy, imagery treatment and interaction — not replacing who you are.

How long does a template redesign take?

A typical redesign takes 3 to 8 weeks depending on the number of page templates, content volume and integrations. Small business sites can be completed faster; larger sites with custom functionality take longer. We agree a schedule before work begins.

Will my site work properly on mobile after the redesign?

Yes. Every redesigned template is built mobile‑first and tested across modern phones, tablets and desktop browsers, so layouts, navigation and forms work smoothly on all screen sizes.

Can you redesign my existing WordPress or CMS template?

Yes. We work with WordPress, Webflow, Shopify, custom CMS platforms and static sites. Where it makes sense we retain your CMS so your team keeps full control of content after launch.
