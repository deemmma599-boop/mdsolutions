who we are

A South African studio focused on growing presence

Makgobatlou Digital Solutions is a South African digital studio built around one idea: your online presence should grow — not age. Too many websites, apps and platforms start strong and slowly lose momentum. They rank less, load slower, look older and convert worse, until someone suggests a rebuild that costs more than the site originally did.

We take a different path. We look at what you have, work out where it is actually losing value, and then help you grow the presence outward — one well-chosen improvement at a time. Sometimes that's SEO. Sometimes it's a redesign. Sometimes it's a performance fix or a mobile app. Most of the time it's a combination, sequenced properly so each improvement builds on the last.

Our clients are individuals and businesses across South Africa: freelancers, creators, small businesses, growing SMEs, and larger teams. What they share is a platform they know deserves better — and a suspicion that the next step should feel clearer than it does.

We are intentionally small, senior and honest. You work directly with the people doing the work. You get written roadmaps, not slide decks. And if a rebuild genuinely isn't necessary, we say so.

Built for how South Africa actually uses the internet

Growing online presence in South Africa means working with the real conditions: mobile-first audiences, variable connectivity, POPIA requirements, load-shedding, and rand-denominated budgets where every rand has to earn its place. We design and build with all of that in mind — not as an afterthought.

SAST working hours

Calls, demos and support run Monday to Friday, 9am–5pm South African Standard Time. No waiting for a UK or US timezone to open.

Rand-based pricing

Quotes, invoices and retainers are in South African Rand. No hidden currency conversions, no offshore rates to justify.

POPIA-aware delivery

Forms, cookie consent, privacy declarations and data handling are designed with the Protection of Personal Information Act in mind.

Mobile-first by default

Most South African traffic arrives on a phone. Everything we design is built for mobile first, then scaled up — not the other way around.

Real-world performance

We optimise for slower connections, mid-range devices and unstable signal — not just for a fast laptop on fibre.

Offline-ready where it matters

For apps used on the move by field teams, we build offline-first functionality so work doesn't stop when the signal does.

About us and growing your online presence

How does Makgobatlou Digital help individuals grow their online presence?

For individuals and solo entrepreneurs, we typically start with a stronger, faster personal website — SEO to help the right people find it, a clean redesign to make the first impression count, and performance work so visitors stay. Later we can add content strategy, app or booking functionality as the presence grows. Engagements are scoped to fit individual budgets, with no minimum business size.

How does Makgobatlou Digital help businesses grow their online presence?

For businesses, we work across the full online presence stack: SEO to be found, redesign to convert, web apps to scale operations, mobile apps for repeat engagement, performance work to keep visitors happy, and strategy to sequence it all. We start with a consultation and roadmap, then deliver the highest-impact work first.

What services does Makgobatlou Digital offer?

SEO optimisation, template redesign, full-stack web app development, Android and iOS app development, performance and Core Web Vitals optimisation, and one-on-one consultation and strategy. Each service can be engaged independently or combined as part of a broader growth plan.

Do you work with individuals as well as businesses in South Africa?

Yes. We work with freelancers, creators, solo entrepreneurs and personal brands as well as small businesses, growing SMEs, larger organisations and non-profits across South Africa. Each engagement is scoped to fit the client's stage, goals and budget.

Where in South Africa do you work?

We work with clients nationwide — including Gauteng, the Western Cape, KwaZulu-Natal, the Eastern Cape and the Free State. Most work is delivered remotely via video calls and shared documents, with in-person sessions arranged by appointment for clients who prefer them.

Do you only work with businesses that already have a website?

No. Most of our work focuses on improving existing platforms, but we also build new websites, web apps and mobile apps from scratch when that is genuinely the right path. If you do not have a site yet, a free consultation will help clarify what you need first and what can wait.

How quickly will I see results growing my online presence?

Design, speed and conversion improvements are often visible within weeks. SEO changes typically show meaningful movement in 3 to 6 months, depending on competition and the site's current health. App and web-app builds take 8 to 16 weeks depending on scope. We are honest about timelines up front.

Is your work POPIA compliant?

Yes. All work we deliver — websites, apps, forms and data handling — is designed with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) in mind, from privacy declarations and cookie consent to secure storage of personal information and adherence to data-minimisation principles.
